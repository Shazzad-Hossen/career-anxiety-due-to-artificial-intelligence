# Career Anxiety Due to Artificial Intelligence

![Project Thumbnail](thumbnail.png)

## 📌 Overview
This notebook explores **career anxiety caused by the rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI)** using data visualization techniques. It focuses on understanding how people perceive AI’s impact on job security, skill relevance, and future career prospects.

The project aims to turn raw data into **clear and meaningful visual insights** that highlight trends, concerns, and patterns related to AI-driven career uncertainty.

---

## 📊 What This Notebook Does
- Cleans and preprocesses the dataset  
- Analyzes factors related to career anxiety, such as:
  - Job security  
  - Fear of automation  
  - Career confidence  
- Visualizes trends and distributions for better interpretability  

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Source
The dataset used in this project was **collected and published by the author** on **Mendeley Data**.

- **Dataset Title:** Career Anxiety Due to Artificial Intelligence  
- **Publisher:** Author  
- **Platform:** Mendeley Data  
- **Link:** https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/r3xh8sd764/2  

The dataset contains survey-based responses examining perceptions of AI, automation, and their impact on career-related anxiety.
